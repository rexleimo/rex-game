# Oracle-bone reference corpus notices

## Chinese-Traditional-Culture/JiaGuWen

- Upstream: <https://github.com/Chinese-Traditional-Culture/JiaGuWen>
- Imported artifacts: the `index.json` modern-character labels and the matching
  50px image samples under `i/`.
- License: the upstream README declares MIT. Retain this notice and the upstream
  attribution when redistributing imported images or the generated catalog.
- Product use: browse-only reference layer. Upstream labels are shown as
  "upstream reading labels" and are not automatically treated as settled
  interpretations or used to manufacture explanations, dates, or quiz answers.

## HUST-OBC (not bundled)

- Dataset page: <https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.25040543.v3>
- License shown by Figshare: CC BY 4.0.
- The dataset documents 1,781 unmerged oracle-bone categories, but its archive is
  about 608 MB. It is registered as a future source candidate rather than being
  silently committed to the web bundle. Importing it requires an explicit asset
  selection, source-to-label audit, and attribution pass.

## Excluded font source

The "古联甲骨文摹写字库" page at the Yin Ruins Oracle Bone Database states
that the font is limited to use with that database product. It is therefore not
vendored or used as a webfont in this project.

## Shanhai-wenshou art pipeline (self-made default, Kenney CC0 override live)

- Default art: `public/assets/shanhai-wenshou/pixel/` generated by
  `scripts/gen-wenshou-pixels.mjs` (CC0, self-made ink-pixel). No upstream.
- Live override: `public/assets/shanhai-wenshou/override/` (manifest enabled),
  synthesised by `scripts/apply-kenney-override.mjs` from vendored sources in
  `resources/kenney-src/new-platformer/`:
  - Kenney New Platformer Pack 1.1 (https://kenney.nl/assets/new-platformer-pack,
    CC0-1.0): player rig, 8 beasts, terrain bricks ×6 biomes, water, torch, door,
    rock, mushroom, gem, sign. No attribution required; recorded here voluntarily.
- Kept self-made (no Kenney species match): ape ×2, horse, ram, fox
  (`beasts.png` cols 0–3/8–9/16–17/24–25) and all parallax ridges
  (`rf_/rm_/rn_*.png`, per-mountain palette identity).
- itch.io packs: still allowed as future sources (CC0 or CC-BY only, no NC/ND);
  CC-BY packs must add an entry here with pack name, author, source URL.
- Runtime: `src/games/shanhai-wenshou/action/assets.ts` prefers override when
  its manifest is usable (not disabled, CC0/CC-BY, non-empty files),
  otherwise falls back to pixel.
